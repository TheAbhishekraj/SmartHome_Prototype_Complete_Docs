## Local Market Tips
1. **Carry a list** with part numbers — shopkeepers know local equivalents
2. **Test before buying** — most shops have test benches
3. **Bargain** — quoted price is usually 20-30% higher
4. **Buy extras** — 20% spare components (they're cheap, shipping later is expensive)
5. **Get receipts** for warranty on adapters, PSUs
6. **Ask for "ISI marked"** for anything mains-related
